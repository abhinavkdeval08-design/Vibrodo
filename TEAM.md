## Why We're Building Vibrodo

Vibrodo started during late-night coding and study sprints in our college hostels—when existing music platforms either pulled us into algorithmic rabbit holes or made synchronized group listening unnecessarily clunky. We're building the audio companion we wanted on our own desks: AMOLED-dark, local-first, resilient on flaky campus networks, and engineered around deep work.

## How We Engineer

- **Zero-Egress Privacy by Default:** Personal device audio files (`FLAC`, `WAV`, `ALAC`, `MP3`) are indexed and played strictly locally—never uploaded to external servers.
- **Fail-Soft Resilience:** From playback state machines to WebSocket room reconnection, every layer is designed to degrade gracefully rather than stall user focus.
- **Open-Source Discipline, Protected Core:** Informed by active contributions to CNCF and open-source ecosystems while keeping Vibrodo's core application and realtime sync engines private during active development.
